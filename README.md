# roadtrip-beijing-guiyang

Beijing to Guiyang road trip plan (via Xi'an and Chengdu): 3 legs, one per day, total driving distance ~2,460 km.

## Trip overview

Departure: **Saturday 24 October 2026, 09:00** from Beijing. One leg per day, departing 09:00 every morning.

| Leg | Route | Distance | Driving time | Departure | Expected arrival |
|-----|-------|----------|--------------|-----------|------------------|
| 1 | Beijing → Xi'an | 1,081 km | 11 h 39 m | Sat 24 Oct, 09:00 | ~20:39 same day |
| 2 | Xi'an → Chengdu | 740 km | 8 h 45 m | Sun 25 Oct, 09:00 | ~17:45 same day |
| 3 | Chengdu → Guiyang | 639 km | 6 h 58 m | Mon 26 Oct, 09:00 | ~15:58 same day |

**Total: 2,460 km and 27 h 22 m of driving over 3 days.**

**Estimated arrival in Guiyang: Monday 26 October 2026, ~15:58 China Standard Time**
(that is **00:58 (12:58 AM) Monday 26 October, Pacific Daylight Time** in San Francisco).

> Note: these times are pure driving times. Add extra time for rest stops, meals and toll plazas.
